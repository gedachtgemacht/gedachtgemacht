# gedacht.gemacht.

Small, honest software. Most of it runs entirely in your browser: no account, no server, no tracking, and it keeps working offline. Everything is open source.

Most app interfaces are in German, since many tools solve German everyday problems (utility bills, e-invoices, shift work). The code and the READMEs are in English.

## Developer tools

- **[dogear](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/dogear)**: a local coding agent on Ollama with Claude-Code-style tools, a shared live browser, plan boards and a receipt for every run.
- **[ticket.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/ticket)**: a self-hosted team ticket board, Slack-style threads plus a Trello-style kanban board. Python stdlib and SQLite, no dependencies.

## Formats and codes

- **[wabe.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/wabe)**: a 2D barcode on a hexagonal grid that carries whole files on paper and screens, read by a phone camera in the browser.
- **[mosaik.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/mosaik)**: a readable plain-text format for tiny images and pixel art that renders the same pixels everywhere.
- **[verlauf.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/verlauf)**: turns a WhatsApp chat export into a verifiable archive (HTML, plain text, SHA-256 checksums).
- **[welk.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/welk)**: checks a folder for file formats at risk of becoming unreadable, for digital preservation.

## Home and money

- **[zähler.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/zaehler)**: meter readings for electricity, gas and water, and whether your monthly prepayment will cover the bill. [Try it](https://zaehler.gedachtgemacht.app)
- **[vorrat.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/vorrat)**: expiry date tracker for fridge and pantry, with barcode scanner. [Try it](https://vorrat.gedachtgemacht.app)
- **[beleg.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/beleg)**: photograph receipts and keep return, warranty and guarantee deadlines in view. [Try it](https://beleg.gedachtgemacht.app)
- **[rechnung.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/rechnung)**: write, check and read German e-invoices (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD) in the browser.
- **[übergabe.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/uebergabe)**: apartment handover protocol on your phone, with photos, signatures and PDF.

## Everyday

- **[schicht.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/schicht)**: shift calendar for rotating shifts, with team view, swaps and ICS export.
- **[strich.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/strich)**: drinks tally sheet for shared flats, clubs and offices.
- **[saat.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/saat)**: sowing calendar and garden planner based on your frost dates.
- **[rucksack.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/rucksack)**: packing list with gear weights and base weight for hiking.
- **[zuschnitt.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/zuschnitt)**: cut list planner for sheets and boards, with saw kerf and printable plans.
- **[riegel.](https://github.com/gedachtgemacht/riegel)**: app and website blocker for Android, on-device, with a local DNS filter.
